C4Y Classroom

An AI instructor and learning management system I helped build at C4Y.

Building the instructor into the learning platform

I worked on AI speaker notes and two ways for learners to interact with the instructor. Text chat used chunking and animation to present responses as they arrived. The voice experience used WebRTC for live conversations.

My work also covered APIs, authentication, and payments. Building those alongside the instructor meant working across the learner’s experience and the application services that supported it.
