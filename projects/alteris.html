Alteris

An online game concept that combines a Roblox-inspired world with life simulation.

Combining a 3D world with life simulation

Alteris explored how an online game world could support the kinds of choices associated with life simulators. The concept paired a 3D world players could explore with choices about the lives of their characters.

The technical stack included React and TypeScript for the web interface, Three.js for the 3D view, and Python for the backend.
