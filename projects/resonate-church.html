Resonate Church

Dashboards and reporting for church service attendance and community outreach.

Comparing the same periods year over year

Website traffic, social engagement, and Connect Card referrals answer different questions about how people find and connect with the church. The reporting dashboard brings those signals into one place and keeps the metrics distinct.

I’m building the dashboard and workflows around a monthly report for church leadership. Comparing each metric with the same period the prior year gives the reader a point of reference beyond the current month’s total.
