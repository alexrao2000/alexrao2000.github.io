FinBull

An agentic research tool that brings market trends and news into one place.

Putting market activity next to the news

FinBull combines two inputs for market research. Market data shows what moved, while news provides events and context for further investigation. Keeping both in the same workflow helps a reader follow a lead without starting a separate search.

The project uses a React and TypeScript interface, a Python FastAPI backend, and PostgreSQL. The application was built with AWS infrastructure.
