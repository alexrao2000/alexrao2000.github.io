Browser extensions · Small useful things

Steam Wishlist Guard

A Chrome extension for a small annoyance: accidentally removing a game from your Steam wishlist and forgetting what it was. It keeps a local removal log and lets you restore entries from the popup.

It catches removal requests as they happen, then compares periodic wishlist snapshots to catch changes made on other devices. Confirmation prompts help prevent mistakes, while duplicate detection keeps the two tracking paths from logging the same removal twice.
