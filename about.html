About

A little about me.

I’m Alex, an adaptable, forward-looking software developer. I’m always chasing that eureka moment when a problem clicks and I see a way forward.

I learn to love things before I do things I love. For me, interest grows through spending time with something, asking questions, and getting past the parts I don’t understand yet. That’s what keeps me willing to take on unfamiliar work.

I’m drawn to what new tools and ideas make possible, and I like finding out by building with them. That approach has taken me from helping build C4Y Classroom from the ground up to working on AI agents, integration workflows, and developer tools at CurieTech. My own projects range from mobile apps and research tools to games and command-line utilities.

I studied computer science and economics at Berkeley. Both shape the questions I ask about a project—how it works, who it helps, and what would make it worth using. I’m happy to learn a new language, tool, or field to find a better answer.
