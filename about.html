About

Product thinking, systems curiosity.

I’m a full-stack developer who enjoys connecting the technical pieces behind a useful product—from the interface and APIs to AI integrations and deployment.

My work has moved between early-stage product building and platform engineering. At C4Y, I helped build an AI instructor and unified LMS from the ground up. At CurieTech, I work on agent platforms, integration workflows, and the infrastructure that helps teams ship them reliably.

My computer science and economics background shapes how I approach engineering: understand the user and the system around a problem, then choose a practical way to solve it.

I’ve also built across a wide range of interests—from mobile apps and market research tools to interactive games, secure file sharing, and command-line utilities.
